Whisperer is a watchOS app (with an optional iPhone companion) for chatting with AI from your wrist. Speak into the watch, get Whisper transcription, stream ChatGPT responses, and hear replies via text-to-speech. It also supports image generation, OCR from photos, language and voice settings, and Base16 theming.

API keys stay off-device via a Vercel proxy. Chat history lives on the device. Whisperer Plus is an optional subscription for Plus features. Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are published on this site for App Store review.
